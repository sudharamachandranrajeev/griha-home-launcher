What's new

- Themes reach the lock screen. With a theme in use and no lock design of your own, the lock screen wears the theme: its picture or page, its lettering and its colours.
- Your own lock design keeps your photo and its subject exactly as they are. Under a theme the date takes the theme's colour, shaded just enough to read on your photo; turn this off in Lock screen, Theme.
- Subject styling: an optional, subtle touch of the theme's colour on the person or object in your lock photo. Choose the theme's pick, Untouched, Colour wash, Duotone, Edge light or Halo behind, and set how strong it is. Each catalogue theme has its own pick.
