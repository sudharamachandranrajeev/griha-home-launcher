What's new

- Themes you can download: Settings, Themes. Get more themes shows the catalogue from griha-themes with previews; download a theme, use it, or remove it. Themes are data only and are checked against their published SHA-256 before use. Each theme brings its own home, not just colours: Pastel Neo (clock card, week pill, tall meters), Candy Gloss (glossy state-coloured pills and a jelly dock), Lime Blob (ring gauges, dot list, sideways motto), Tangerine Peel (info panel, well clock, badge dock), Clay Bloom (clay card grid with a bottom bar), Midnight Jelly (a dark dashboard with tabs, stats, toasts and glowing steps), Berry Wave (tactile pill keys) and Teal Grid (a teal styleguide with chips, tabs, a status card and alerts). Turn a theme's home off in Settings, Home screen.
- Soft glass redesigned: a frosted clock card, chips, clear pill keys with coloured rims, a glass dock and a bottom bar.
- Wallpapers drawn by Griha behind home and all apps, the same or separate, that drift as you scroll and answer taps in the theme's style. Android's wallpaper still works too.
- Lock photo blur, rebuilt: soft focus, frosted glass, lens bokeh, progressive, zoom burst and motion, with strength, colour, light and grain.
- All apps: a side alphabet you touch and slide, and All apps 3D (extruded tiles or a 3D wheel).
- Fixed: rows in all apps squeezing, tilting or leaving marks while scrolling.
