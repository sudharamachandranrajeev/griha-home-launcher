What's new

- The theme's clock comes to the lock screen. Each theme's clock piece (Pastel Neo's clock card, Lime Blob's ring gauges, Tangerine Peel's well, Teal Grid's headline, and the rest) appears under Android's own clock in a date edition: Android keeps the live time, the piece shows the day, the month and the week.
- With no lock design of your own, the themed lock screen uses the piece automatically. With your own design, turn on "Show the theme's clock piece instead of my text" in Lock screen, Theme; with the depth effect on, your photo's subject stands in front of it.
- Berry Wave shows its pill keys and Midnight Jelly its stat cards. Update the themes from Settings, Themes to get these.
